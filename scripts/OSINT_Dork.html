📱 Social Media Investigation

📅 Unix Timestamp Investigation

Important: Social media platforms like Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram use Unix timestamps internally for post timing. Unix time is seconds since January 1, 1970 (UTC).

Why use Unix time? It provides precise timing for posts, especially useful for timeline reconstruction and forensic analysis.
